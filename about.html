About CollegeHub.

Created: 21 November 2026 Last updated: 21 November 2026
01

What is CollegeHub?

CollegeHub is a student workspace designed to help make college life more organised. It brings useful academic tools together in one place, allowing students to manage their work, keep track of deadlines and monitor their progress.

The aim is to provide a straightforward platform that makes everyday student organisation easier.

02

Our Purpose

CollegeHub was created to make managing college responsibilities simpler. With assignments, coursework and deadlines to keep track of, it can be difficult to stay organised.

By bringing these tools together, CollegeHub aims to help students stay on top of their responsibilities and manage their time more effectively.

03

What Can You Do?

CollegeHub includes several features designed around everyday student needs:

  • Assignments: Keep track of assignments and their deadlines.
  • Coursework: Organise coursework and monitor its progress.
  • Reminders: Keep important dates and tasks in mind.
  • Progress: View your academic progress in one place.
  • Student Profiles: Manage your account and personal workspace details.
04

Who Is It For?

CollegeHub is intended for students who want a simple way to organise their academic responsibilities.

Whether you are managing several assignments, planning upcoming coursework or keeping track of deadlines, the platform is designed to bring those tasks together.

05

Behind the Project

CollegeHub was created by Ignacy Wickramanayake as a small, independent personal project.

The idea was to develop a useful student workspace while exploring website development, database management, authentication and online security.

It is not an official college system and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any college or educational institution. The platform is currently intended for personal use and sharing with a small group of users.

06

Security and Development

Security has been considered throughout the development of CollegeHub. The project includes account authentication, database access controls and user-specific data permissions.

An internal security review was carried out to check important security settings and access controls. This was a project-level review, not a professional penetration test or guarantee that the platform is free from vulnerabilities.
