The rules and responsibilities for using the CollegeHub platform.

Terms of Use Last updated: 21 November 2026
01

Introduction

These Terms of Use explain the rules for accessing and using CollegeHub, a personal student portal project shared with a limited group of invited users.

02

Acceptable Use

Users agree to:

  • Use CollegeHub for its intended educational and organisational purposes.
  • Keep their login credentials private.
  • Provide accurate information where reasonably possible.
  • Respect other users and their information.
  • Use the platform responsibly.
03

Account Security

Users are responsible for keeping their accounts secure and should notify the project owner if they suspect unauthorised access.

04

Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

  • Attempt to access another user's account.
  • Attempt to bypass security controls or permissions.
  • Interfere with the operation of CollegeHub.
  • Misuse or share another user's personal information.
  • Upload harmful or malicious content.
05

User Content

Users are responsible for the information and coursework they enter into CollegeHub. Users should only upload content they are permitted to use.

06

Availability

CollegeHub is a personal project and is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability. Features may change, become unavailable or be removed.

07

Account Suspension

Access may be restricted or an account may be suspended if a user misuses the platform or compromises its security.

08

Educational Purpose

CollegeHub is an organisational tool. It is not an official college system and does not replace college platforms, teachers or official academic records.

09

Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as the project develops. Updated terms should be made available to users.

10

Contact

Users can contact the project owner directly with questions about these terms or the use of CollegeHub.
