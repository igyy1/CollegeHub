COLLEGEHUB DOCUMENTATION

Your privacy matters.

Find out how CollegeHub collects, stores and uses your information.

Privacy Policy Last updated: 21 November 2026
01

Introduction

CollegeHub is a personal student portal project designed to help a small group of students organise assignments, coursework, reminders and academic progress.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used and how it is stored.

02

Information We Collect

CollegeHub may store the following information:

  • Email addresses used to create accounts.
  • Display names provided by users.
  • College and course information entered into profiles.
  • Assignments, coursework and reminders created by users.
  • Account roles and account creation dates.
03

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate CollegeHub and provide its features, including account authentication, personal profiles and academic organisation tools.

04

Data Storage

CollegeHub uses Supabase for account authentication and database storage. Information is stored using Supabase's hosted infrastructure.

05

Information Sharing

CollegeHub is intended for a small group of invited users. Personal information is not intended to be publicly displayed or sold.

Administrators may have access to certain account information for website management. Access depends on the permissions configured within the application.

06

Account Security

CollegeHub uses authentication and database access controls to help protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

07

User Responsibilities

Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into CollegeHub. Users should also keep their login credentials private.

08

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and website information may remain stored until it is deleted by an authorised administrator or through an available account management process.

Users can contact the project owner to request assistance with accessing or deleting their data.

09

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when CollegeHub changes or introduces new features.

10

Contact

For questions about this policy or information stored by CollegeHub, contact the project owner directly.
