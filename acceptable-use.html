Use CollegeHub responsibly.

Guidelines for keeping the platform safe, respectful and reliable.

Acceptable Use Policy Last updated: 21 November 2026
01

Purpose

This policy explains how users are expected to behave when using CollegeHub. It aims to maintain a safe, respectful and reliable environment.

02

Account Responsibilities

  • Only use your own account.
  • Keep your password private.
  • Log out when using a shared computer.
  • Report suspicious account activity.
03

Respect for Other Users

  • Do not attempt to view another user's private coursework.
  • Do not interfere with another user's account or information.
  • Do not use the platform to harass or intimidate others.
  • Respect the privacy of everyone using CollegeHub.
04

Security and Technical Misuse

Users must not:

  • Attempt to bypass authentication or database security.
  • Attempt to gain administrator privileges without permission.
  • Exploit software vulnerabilities.
  • Disrupt or damage the website or its services.
  • Introduce malicious scripts or files.
05

Content Restrictions

Users must not upload or submit content that is unlawful, threatening, abusive, malicious or infringes another person's rights.

06

Personal Information

Users should not enter passwords, financial details, medical information or other highly sensitive personal information into CollegeHub.

07

Reporting Problems

Users should contact the project owner if they notice suspicious activity, inappropriate use, security problems or technical issues.

08

Consequences of Misuse

Breaches of this policy may result in a warning, temporary suspension or removal of access to CollegeHub.

09

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as CollegeHub develops. Users should review the latest version when changes are made.
